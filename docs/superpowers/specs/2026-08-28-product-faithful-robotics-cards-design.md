# Product-Faithful Robotics Cards Design

**Date:** 2026-08-28

## Objective

Replace the generic humanoid figures in the Leju Kuavo and PM01 robotics card artwork with faithful depictions of the actual products Zetrix sells, while preserving the established premium Zetrix card treatment.

## Source Assets

- Leju Kuavo edit target: `docs/robotics-cards/source/zetrix-robotics-leju-kuavo-friendly-v2.png`
- PM01 edit target: `docs/robotics-cards/source/zetrix-robotics-pm01.png`
- Kuavo-MY product reference: `https://www.lejurobot.com/en/products/kuavo-my`
- PM01 product reference: `https://www.humanoid-robots.io/robot/pm01-by-engineai`

The product-reference imagery controls robot identity and hardware. The existing card images control composition, atmosphere, floating-symbol treatment, and visual family.

## Product Fidelity

### Kuavo-MY

Depict the actual Kuavo-MY design: rounded glossy black sensor visor and crown, white lower face shell, white-and-black torso, exposed dark arm mechanisms, articulated grey hands, authentic proportions, and recognizable joint construction. Do not replace these features with smooth generic sci-fi armor, a black faceplate with red eyes, or an invented illuminated chest core.

### PM01

Depict the actual EngineAI PM01 design: compact gunmetal/silver chassis, layered metallic sensor head with horizontal camera bands, dark neck and torso structure, orange-red chest interface, circular joint housings, authentic proportions, and recognizable mechanical construction. Do not replace these features with white ceramic armor, a generic glossy faceplate, or invented humanoid anatomy.

Manufacturer wordmarks and readable product text are omitted to prevent distorted generated lettering. Their absence must not alter the recognizable hardware design.

## Shared Art Direction

- Premium cinematic 3D product visualization.
- Portrait composition matching the current source aspect ratio.
- Consistent dark graphite studio/laboratory background across both images.
- Cool neutral key light with controlled Zetrix-red rim and accent illumination.
- Full robot remains legible inside the central safe crop with grounded contact shadows.
- Lower region remains comparatively quiet for the existing glass caption treatment.
- No text, letters, logos, watermark, extra robot, or invented branded hardware.

## Card-Specific Treatment

### Kuavo-MY Card

Use a warm, professional, open-hand interaction pose appropriate to commercial service and embodied intelligence. Retain the existing dimensional workflow structure, floating technology markers, orbital arcs, and restrained red motion trails. The icons remain secondary to the real product.

### PM01 Card

Use a confident, research-oriented pose that communicates agility and embodied-AI development. Retain the floating BrainCircuit and Bot symbols, sparse red particle lattice, and articulated joint-ring accents. The icons remain secondary to the real product.

## Output and Acceptance Criteria

- Save final PNG sources at the two existing user-named source paths, replacing the obsolete generic-robot images.
- Preserve the original files' portrait orientation and produce the highest practical resolution from the built-in image workflow.
- Both cards read as one coherent set through background, lighting, palette, rendering quality, and icon materials.
- A viewer familiar with each supplied product reference can identify Kuavo-MY and PM01 from their hardware without relying on labels.
- No generic white Zetrix mascot remains in either card.
- No malformed text or manufacturer logo is visible.

## Verification

Visually inspect both generated images at full frame and reduced card scale. Confirm product-specific head, torso, joint, hand, and material details; matching background and lighting; preserved floating-icon treatment; safe portrait framing; and absence of text, logos, watermarks, or hybridized robot features.
